NP Membership benefits

Designed for nonprofit organizations contributing to their communities.

  • No annual membership fee.
  • Attend selected networking events at no charge.
  • Access member learning resources and community updates.

Bronze Membership benefits

A practical starting point for organizations building new business connections.

  • Member directory listing.
  • Invitations to networking events.
  • Discounted registration for selected workshops.

Silver Membership benefits

For growing organizations looking for more ways to learn and be seen.

  • All Bronze benefits.
  • Priority access to selected training sessions.
  • Eligibility for a rotating website business spotlight.
  • Additional event registration discounts.

Gold Membership benefits

Enhanced engagement for organizations seeking broader visibility.

  • All Silver benefits.
  • Featured placement in selected member promotions.
  • Complimentary entry to selected premium events.
  • Priority consideration for a website business spotlight.
